# Customer Success and Onboarding Playbook

## Onboarding goals

Customer success onboarding is designed to help sales, solutions, security, legal, and proposal teams use the copilot safely. The first onboarding milestone is a cited answer walkthrough using customer-approved source documents and a pilot RFP.

## Training plan

Week one covers workspace setup, source document tagging, SSO or API-key access, dashboard navigation, cited question answering, missing-evidence behavior, and standard eval commands. Week two covers requirement matrices, review board workflows, source request packs, handoff boards, launch checklist artifacts, and executive submission decision memos.

## Success metrics

Onboarding success metrics include active reviewer adoption, percentage of requirements with evidence, citation coverage, eval pass rate, red-team missing-evidence pass rate, source request closure time, and reduction in unsupported RFP claims.

## Customer responsibilities

Customers provide approved source documents, name business owners for pricing and legal exceptions, validate privacy and security claims, attend weekly onboarding sessions, and approve go-live readiness.
