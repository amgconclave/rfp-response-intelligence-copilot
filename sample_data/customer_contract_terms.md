# Acme Procurement Contract Terms

1. Limitation of Liability.
Supplier shall accept unlimited liability for breach, data incidents, confidentiality failures, service outages, and all consequential, indirect, special, and punitive damages arising from this agreement.

2. Data Processing and Subprocessors.
Supplier must process personal data only on Acme instructions, sign Acme's DPA without modification, delete all customer data within 24 hours on request, and obtain written approval before adding or changing any subprocessor.

3. Security Obligations.
Supplier must comply with Acme's customer security policy, maintain SSO, encryption, vulnerability management, and incident response controls, notify Acme of any suspected incident within 24 hours, and remediate all critical vulnerabilities within 48 hours.

4. SLA and Service Credits.
Supplier shall provide 99.99% monthly uptime. Any downtime will trigger uncapped service credits equal to monthly fees, and service credits are not the customer's sole remedy.

5. Audit Rights.
Acme may perform onsite audits at any time, inspect Supplier systems and records, and require third-party audit support without additional confidentiality or scope limits.

6. Termination.
Acme may terminate for convenience without cause on immediate notice after kickoff and receive a pro-rata refund plus wind-down assistance at no additional charge.

7. Indemnity.
Supplier shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Acme against all claims, regulatory fines, customer data claims, security incidents, and third-party claims related to the services.

8. Data Residency.
Supplier must store all production data in the European Union only, prevent cross-border transfers, and guarantee data localization for all subprocessors and model providers.

9. AI and Customer Data Use.
Supplier may not use AI unless Acme approves every model provider. Supplier must also warrant that prompts, logs, vector metadata, and customer data are never used to train models without written approval.

10. Pricing and Payment.
Acme requires net 90 payment terms, most favored customer pricing, price match rights, unlimited users during the pilot, and the right to withhold payment for any unresolved support ticket.
