# SLA and Support Policy

## Support tiers

Standard support covers business-hours triage for local setup, sample corpus ingestion, dashboard usage, eval commands, and artifact generation. Enterprise support adds named customer success management, quarterly workflow review, and prioritized support for production API and dashboard incidents.

## Response targets

Severity 1 production outage response target is 4 business hours for enterprise support. Severity 2 degraded retrieval, dashboard, or eval behavior response target is 1 business day. Severity 3 documentation, onboarding, or non-blocking workflow questions response target is 2 business days.

## Availability posture

The local demo does not provide a contractual uptime guarantee. Production availability targets must be documented in the customer order form and depend on the selected hosting, vector store, identity provider, and optional LLM provider. The support policy can be cited for response targets but not for an unconditional uptime guarantee.

## Escalation

Escalations route to customer success for onboarding issues, security for SSO or audit questions, engineering for API or retrieval defects, and legal for contractual SLA exceptions.
